The Problem TrackIT Solves

OJT management in the CICT department currently relies on paper-based DTRs and manual processes — non-integrated, lacking real-time tracking, report generation, and interactive visualization tools, causing inefficiencies and delays.

Core Features

Everything You Need,
In One System.

Six powerful modules built to eliminate manual OJT inefficiencies for trainees, supervisors, and coordinators alike.

Dynamic QR Attendance

Real-time, secure attendance tracking via Dynamic QR codes with geotagging and camera-based DTR authentication to prevent proxy attendance.

Digital Weekly Journals

Structured checkbox-based journals capturing theoretical concepts in practice, with integrated grammar correction and NLP-powered auto-summarization.

Descriptive Analytics

Interactive dashboards and charts giving coordinators data-driven insights into trainee performance, attendance trends, and program outcomes.

Digital Signature Verification

Supervisors verify and digitally sign weekly journals and monthly performance appraisal forms with a single confirmation — no printing required.

NLP Auto-Summarization

Natural Language Processing automatically generates concise, meaningful summaries of trainee journal entries, saving coordinators hours of review time.

Printable Document Generation

Automatically generate and export printable DTRs, weekly journals, and monthly performance appraisal forms ready for submission and record-keeping.

How It Works

From Check-In to
Certified Report.

A seamless end-to-end workflow that replaces every paper form.

01

Trainee Scans Dynamic QR

Upon arrival at the company, the OJT trainee scans the daily Dynamic QR code. Geotagging confirms the trainee's location for authenticity.

02

Daily Duty Logs & Weekly Journals

Trainees submit virtual duty logs of daily tasks and write weekly journals with checkbox-based theory-to-practice entries and grammar correction.

03

Supervisor Reviews & Signs

Company supervisors verify DTRs and duty logs, then digitally sign the weekly journal and monthly performance appraisal form with a single click.

04

NLP Summarizes Journals

The NLP engine auto-generates concise summaries of trainee journals, allowing OJT coordinators to quickly assess trainee learning outcomes.

05

Analytics, Reports & Exports

Coordinators access descriptive analytics dashboards, generate automated reports, and export printable DTRs and performance appraisal forms for submission.

About the Project

Capstone Project
IT27-E05

TrackIT is a Capstone Project for INTE1031 – Capstone Project 1 developed by Group IT27-E05 at the College of Information and Communications Technology, STI West Negros University, Bacolod City.

The system addresses the research gap of the absence of a centralized, web-based OJT monitoring platform for the CICT department — replacing inefficient manual processes with intelligent, automated solutions.
